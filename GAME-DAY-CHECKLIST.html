Game-Day Checklist

Fishers Tigers Lacrosse Scorebook · drewcollier.github.io/lax_scorebook
Every stat is entered the way it is called: Stat → Jersey # → Time (time only when asked)

1. At the field, 15 minutes before

  • Open the app. Blue "new version" bar? Tap Reload.
  • Settings → Test Connection says connected (if you have signal).
  • Roster → Pull Roster from Sheets (needs the PIN). Starters look right.
  • Device charged (or battery pack). Auto-lock off if possible.
  • Decide who calls stats and who taps.

2. Starting the game

  • Game tab: opponent, date, Home/Away, then Start Game.
  • Fill in officials and weather if known. Leave the rest.
  • By Stat is selected.
  • Keypad or Jersey Grid, whichever you prefer.
  • Quarter shows Q1.

3. Goals

  • Goal → scorer # → OK
  • Assist # → OK, or No Assist
  • Time on the clock, every goal (e.g. 0934 = 9:34)
  • Shot location, or Skip if you missed it
  • A goal also counts as a shot and a shot on goal. Don't enter those separately.

4. Penalties

  • PENALTY → team → player → clock time (enter it carefully) → penalty → duration
  • Personal foul that's non-releasable? Tick Locked in.
  • Penalty over? Tap Served in the Active penalties box.
  • EVEN / MAN-UP / MAN-DOWN update by themselves. Don't tap them.

5. Through the game

  • Change the quarter at every break, including OT.
  • Mistake? Find it in the Event Log and tap Undo (removes everything that tap recorded).
  • App reloaded or screen locked? Just reopen it; the game is kept.
  • "Jersey # doesn't exist"? Tap Re-enter, or Cancel.
  • "Penalty already expired" after a goal? Tap OK and carry on.

6. After the game

  • Tap END GAME, then enter the Stats PIN, or tap Cancel and hand the tablet to the coach.
  • Coach: History → Press to sync → PIN. Wait for Synced ✓.
  • No signal? The game waits in History; sync it later.
  • History → MaxPreps file. Scoresheet: in the team sheet, Lacrosse Tools → Generate Printable Scoresheet.
  • Jot down anything confusing and pass it on.
Don't stop to fix things during play. Keep entering stats in order. Use Undo at the next stoppage, and tap Served for any penalty you forgot when you notice it.
